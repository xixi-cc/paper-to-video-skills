# How to cite Paper to Video Skills

Please cite the exact release tag or commit used so the workflow can be
reproduced.

中文推荐格式：

> 白拾的物理AI组会、曹溪能（Xixi Cao）及贡献者，《Paper to Video Skills：
> Codex 适配版》，[版本或提交哈希]，2026，
> https://github.com/xixi-cc/paper-to-video-skills。

English:

> 白拾的物理AI组会, Cao, Xineng (Xixi Cao), and contributors. *Paper to
> Video Skills: Codex adaptation*. Version [release or commit], 2026.
> https://github.com/xixi-cc/paper-to-video-skills.

When citing an output such as a slide deck or video, cite that output and its
underlying paper separately. The repository citation documents the tooling;
it does not transfer authorship of generated research explanations or of the
source paper.
